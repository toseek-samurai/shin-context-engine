# v2.0.5-dev.121

Preview candidate. Each platform is published after its source and exact archive
pass qualification in CI.

## Changes

- Share context delivery between one-shot and resident readers. Source views
  retain a structured delivery receipt alongside their bounded text; summaries
  consume that receipt directly. Query, inspect and read responses keep their
  existing schemas, snapshot bindings, source locators and output budgets.
- Share source-cache completion and the final current-snapshot check across
  canonical and bootstrap preparation. A successful publication qualification
  includes its completed cache transfer; completion does not rebuild that cache.
  Keep route approvals, binding saves, qualification receipts and granted
  version pins under their existing authority rules.
- Exercise omitted fragments, retained partial context, unknown delivery counts
  and resource warnings through the common delivery module. Preserve source,
  freshness, safety and failure-ordering checks in the callers.
- Bring installation and platform guides up to the qualified dev.120 download
  baseline. Put installation and agent setup earlier in README, make the native
  screenshots easier to read, and add a domain glossary to the self profile.
  Homebrew retains its independently qualified version and update lifecycle.

## Qualification scope

The MCP adapter contract and persisted formats are unchanged. Project authority,
profile approvals and resource limits remain enforced. New kits
continue to rebuild their owned derived indexes while preserving project sources,
enrollment, approvals, agent settings and history.

Local paired native measurements used synthetic corpora on macOS. They do not
establish a general speedup or qualify another platform or installed kit.
Upper-tail index-refresh latency remains an open diagnostic follow-up.

Core is `2.0.5-dev.28`. Observer, CLI, MCP, Monitor and Updater are `2.0.5-dev.121`.
