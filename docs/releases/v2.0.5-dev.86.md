# v2.0.5-dev.86

Preview candidate. Each platform becomes available only after its own source
and exact-archive CI qualification passes. Pending macOS ARM runners do not
block qualified Linux or Windows downloads.

- Restore the original dark Sand palette while retaining Overview, Projects,
  History and Settings, labeled actions and disclosed technical details.
- Use a compact 520 px standard panel width, 14 px body text, smaller headings
  and spacing, and project lists sized to their visible rows.
- Fit short Overview, Projects and Settings pages to their contents, up to a
  680 px standard or 560 px compact ceiling. Long activity and detail views
  retain bounded height and scrolling. A fixed layout budget preserves fonts
  and pagination as the native popup changes height.
- Resize the retained macOS tray popover after Metal rendering, preserving its
  content owner, status-item anchor, focus, clicks, reopen and compact behavior.
  Windows resizes after presentation, preserving its bottom anchor, focus and
  Z-order while respecting monitor bounds and DPI.
- Keep warning details available when entering Overview from a short page on
  a smaller screen. Cover automatic-height transitions in EN/RU and preserve
  the existing fixed-size render matrix.
- Update dark Sand setup and updater colors without changing their reviewed
  installation, progress, saved-result or recovery flows.
- Refresh all fifteen native macOS/Windows documentation screenshots and their
  capture provenance. Terminal navigation and appearance remain unchanged.

Local checks include macOS fixed and fitted Metal renders, native popup
focus/resize behavior, Windows native and headless rendering, 100/150/200% DPI,
exact fixed-frame totals and separate adaptive-height checks. These do not
replace guarded CI qualification of the exact release source and packages,
or establish a new installed MSI lifecycle test.

Core remains `2.0.5-dev.15`; Observer, CLI, MCP, Monitor and Updater are
`2.0.5-dev.86`. Generator remains `shin-generator-v24-objective-cpp`.
No project profile scope, service limit or test deadline is increased.

Download pins remain on a published qualified preview until new platform
assets are public. Previously published tags and assets are preserved.
